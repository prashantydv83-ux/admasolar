Frequently asked questions.

The questions we hear most often — savings, subsidy, on-grid vs hybrid vs off-grid, install, and AMC.

Getting started

Is solar right for my home?

Usually yes if you have space and sunlight. We start from your bill, roof, shade, and future loads.

How much rooftop space do I need?

About 80–100 sq. ft. of shadow-free area per kW.

On-grid, hybrid, or off-grid?

On-grid for bill cut and CFA. Hybrid if you also need backup. Off-grid where the grid is missing.

Will on-grid work in a power cut?

No — anti-islanding shuts it down. Choose hybrid with battery for backup.

Cost & subsidy

How much subsidy can I get?

Residential CFA: ₹30,000 (1 kW), ₹60,000 (2 kW), ₹78,000 (3 kW+), subject to MNRE rules.

Does ADMA handle DISCOM and CFA?

Yes — documentation, portal filing, and tracking until credit. Final release stays with the authorities.

Are EMI options available?

Zero-down EMI from about ₹1,499/month may be available through bank/NBFC partners.

Will I still get an electricity bill?

Usually yes — fixed charges remain. Solar can cut the unit bill sharply; it does not guarantee a zero bill every month.

Install & care

How long does install take?

Physical residential install is often a few days; 48-hour install after layout approval on standard roofs.

How much maintenance?

Low. Periodic cleaning and checks. AMC is available.

Do panels work on cloudy days?

Yes, at lower output. They do not generate at night — hybrid batteries cover that.
